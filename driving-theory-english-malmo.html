What is the Swedish theory test?

The Swedish driving theory test (kunskapsprov) is a mandatory exam you must pass before the practical driving test. It has 70 multiple-choice questions — 65 are scored and 5 are pilot questions that don't affect your result. Each question has four answer options and only one is correct, and you can move between questions and change your answers throughout the test. To pass, you need at least 52 correct out of the 65 scored questions (80%), within 50 minutes. The test is available in multiple languages, including English and Arabic.

You take the test on a computer at Trafikverket's driving-test office at Cederströmsgatan 1 in Malmö. Before the test you show ID. When you finish, you see your result immediately on screen and also receive it by email. If you don't pass, you're told which subject areas you got wrong — so you know exactly what to focus on before you retake it.

A passed theory test is valid for 4 months. If you don't pass the practical driving test within that window, you'll need to retake the theory — so plan your timeline and book your practical test shortly after passing the theory.

What does the test cover?

The Swedish theory test is built around five official subject areas:

  • Traffic rules – road signs, road markings and traffic signals
  • Traffic safety – safe behaviour and risk awareness in traffic
  • Vehicle knowledge – the car's technology, controls and maintenance
  • Environment – eco-friendly driving and fuel economy
  • Personal conditions – fatigue, attitudes, vision and health
