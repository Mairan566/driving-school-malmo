What is the MC theory test?

To get a motorcycle licence in Sweden (A1, A2 or A), you must pass a theory test at Trafikverket (the Swedish Transport Administration) before taking the practical driving exam. The theory test covers traffic rules, road signs, risk awareness and motorcycle-specific topics such as balance, road positioning and visibility.

The test has 70 questions in total — 5 unscored pilot questions plus 65 scored questions — and you need at least 52 correct (80%) to pass. The test duration is 50 minutes. Important: the MC theory test is only in Swedish. If you do not speak Swedish, you can request an oral test (muntligt prov) with an interpreter at Trafikverket. We help you prepare in English so every concept is clear before the test.

The 5 categories of the MC theory test

Vehicle knowledge

The motorcycle's technology and function: engine, brakes, transmission, tyres, suspension and electrical system. Understanding how your MC works helps you ride safely and spot faults.

Environment

Fuel consumption, emissions and eco-friendly riding. How to ride fuel-efficiently, reduce emissions and lower your environmental impact through the way you ride.

Traffic safety

Risks specific to motorcyclists: visibility, braking distances, cornering technique, grip and the importance of protective gear. How to protect yourself and others.

Traffic rules

Road signs, traffic signals, right-of-way rules, speed limits and other regulations — the fundamental rules every rider must know.

Personal factors

Fatigue, alcohol, drugs, medication, stress and peer pressure — how personal factors affect your ability to ride a motorcycle safely.

Important information

The theory test is the first step on your journey to a motorcycle licence. You must pass the theory test before you can book the practical riding test at Trafikverket. The test is taken on a computer at the Trafikverket centre at Cederströmsgatan 1, 212 39 Malmö.

The motorcycle theory test is different from the car theory test. While there is overlap in general traffic rules, the motorcycle test includes specific questions about balance, braking, protective gear, road positioning and riding techniques that do not appear on the car test. Even if you have passed the car theory test, you must pass the motorcycle theory test separately.

After you pass the theory test

Once you pass the MC theory test, your result is registered with Trafikverket and a passed theory test is valid for 1 year. You must take and pass the practical riding test within that year — if you don't, you'll need to retake the theory test.

Before you can book the practical riding exam, you also need to have completed the motorcycle risk training — Riskettan (Risk 1) and Risktvåan MC (Risk 2) — and hold a valid learner's permit (körkortstillstånd).
